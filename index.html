The Brand

Not another
basic brand.

SHIFT was born in Kolkata from a simple frustration: most menswear either feels outdated or tries too hard. We wanted pieces that actually fit the way Gen-Z lives — relaxed, expressive, and effortless.

We start with shirts. No noise. Elevated fits, better fabrics, and designs that feel current. As the culture moves, so does the brand.

This is menswear that respects your time and your style — built to shift with you.
